What is fika?

A coffee break, taken seriously.

Fika is the Swedish habit of stopping for coffee and something sweet, with friends or on your own. It isn't about rushing a caffeine fix. It's about taking ten minutes for yourself.

That's what we're here for. Grab a latte on your way to work, or slow down with a matcha after dinner. We're open until midnight.

Coffee classics, done right

Espresso, long black, latte, cappuccino and flat white, pulled to order.

Matcha & tea series

Strawberry, hazelnut, yuzu and peach matcha, plus a full range of hot and iced teas.

Easy to grab and go

Dine in or take away. Quick enough for the morning commute.
